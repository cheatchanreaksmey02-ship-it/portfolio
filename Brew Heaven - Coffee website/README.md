# Brew Heaven — Coffee Website

A UI/UX design for a coffee shop e-commerce website, designed in Figma. The design covers the full customer journey — from browsing the menu to checking out.

## Screens included

- **Home** — hero banner, featured coffee and dessert menu, customer reviews
- **Create Account / Login** — with social sign-up options (Facebook, Google)
- **About Us** — brand story and mission
- **Basket / Checkout** — cart summary, delivery details, and payment
